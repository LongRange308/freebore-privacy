Freebore privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Freebore is a logbook app for rifle shooters. It is built to keep your information on your own phone.

What Freebore collects

Nothing. Freebore has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send any information to the developer or to anyone else.

Information stored on your phone

The rifles, barrels, suppressors, range sessions, dope cards, groups, notes and photos you enter are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Camera and photos

Freebore uses the camera, or the system photo picker, only when you choose to add a photo of a rifle or a target. Photos are copied into the app's own storage on your device and are never uploaded. The app does not read your photo library beyond the photos you pick.

Sharing

Information leaves your phone only when you choose to share it, for example a backup file, a spreadsheet export or a PDF dope card, through your phone's share sheet. Where it goes is up to you.

Children

Freebore is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
